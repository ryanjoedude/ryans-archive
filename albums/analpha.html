Analphabetapolothology

Cap'n Jazz

Released: 01/08/1998

Analphabetapolothology is an anthology compilation album by Cap'n Jazz released in 1998 on Jade Tree Records. It collects nearly all recordings by the band, including studio tracks, live recordings, EP material, and unreleased demos.
