Mingus Ah Um

Charles Mingus

Released: 08/1959

Mingus Ah Um is a studio album by American jazz musician Charles Mingus which was released in October 1959 by Columbia Records.[1][2] It was his first album recorded for Columbia. The title is a corruption of an imaginary Latin declension.
